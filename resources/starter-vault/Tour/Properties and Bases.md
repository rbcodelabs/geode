# Properties and Bases

Sometimes you want a note to carry facts as well as prose, such as a status, a rating or a date. Those go in **properties**, the small table at the top of a note.

Open [[How to Take Smart Notes]] and you'll see `author`, `status` and `rating` at the top.

## Try it

- [ ] Open [[Building a Second Brain]] and change its `status` from `want to read` to `reading`.
