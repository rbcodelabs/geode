# Make it yours

This vault is yours now. Keep what's useful and delete the rest. Nothing here is special: it's all ordinary files.

## Clear out the tour

- [ ] Right-click the **Tour** folder in the file list and delete it. [[Start here]] can go too.

The `Reading/` and `Ideas/` notes and the reading list are real, working examples. Keep them as a starting point or delete them.

## Where your files are

Right-click any note and choose **Reveal in Finder**. That folder is your whole vault: back it up, sync it, or open it in another editor. Geode keeps its own settings in a hidden `.geode` folder and never changes your notes on its own.

## Want the tour again?

Press `Cmd+P` and search **Learn Geode**.

## Coming from Obsidian?

Open your existing Obsidian vault with *Open folder as vault*. Your notes, links and properties work as they are.
