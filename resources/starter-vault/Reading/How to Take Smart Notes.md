# How to Take Smart Notes

A book about the slip-box (*Zettelkasten*) method used by sociologist Niklas Luhmann. The core argument is that writing isn't a final step after thinking. Writing *is* the thinking, and a collection of connected notes does much of the work for you.

## What stuck with me

- Take notes [[Write in your own words|in your own words]], not as copied highlights.
- [[Notes should connect]]: each new note should link to ones you already have.
- Let topics emerge from the links rather than deciding categories up front.
