# The Extended Mind

The argument is that we think with more than our brains: with our bodies, our surroundings and the tools we use. Getting ideas out of your head and onto a page frees you to work with them.

## Notes so far

- Offloading thoughts to paper or screen isn't a crutch. It changes what you can think about. See [[Write in your own words]].
- Arranging ideas in space helps you see relationships. That's the case for a [[Map of this vault.canvas|canvas]].
